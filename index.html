How it works

A clear path, four steps long

Immigration is a staged process. We make each stage predictable, so you always know what's happening and what comes next.

01

Free assessment

A 30-minute call to understand your goals and confirm which visa pathways are realistically open to you.

02

Strategy & plan

A written roadmap covering your best pathway, timeline, costs and the documents you'll need to gather.

03

Application

We prepare, check and lodge your application, and handle all correspondence with Immigration New Zealand.

04

Arrival & beyond

Decision in hand, we help with next steps — from visa conditions to your eventual path to residence or citizenship.
