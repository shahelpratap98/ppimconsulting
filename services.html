Our services

Every visa pathway to New Zealand, under one roof

Whether you're moving for work, love, study or business, our licensed advisers match you to the right pathway and carry the application from start to decision.

Skilled Migrant Category residence

New Zealand's main residence pathway for qualified professionals and skilled tradespeople. We calculate your points, identify how to strengthen your claim, and manage your Expression of Interest and residence application end to end.

  • Points assessment
  • EOI lodgement
  • Residence application

Accredited Employer Work Visa (AEWV)

The standard work visa for people with a job offer from an accredited NZ employer. We coordinate with your employer, verify the job check, and prepare a complete, well-evidenced application — minimising back-and-forth with Immigration NZ.

  • Job offer review
  • Visa application
  • Condition variations

Partnership & family visas

Join a partner, parent or child who lives in New Zealand. Relationship-based applications live or die on evidence, so we build your file to the exact standard Immigration NZ case officers expect — before it's ever lodged.
